(for the map asset, small adventure is the candy map and level1 is factory)

Main screen: there will be a main screen that let player choose the start the game(default map will be candy) or check the levels in Level select.

Level Select: Player can check the levels in the game and select the one that they want to play.

Lose screen: when player died, the lose screen will shows u, the player can either restart/retry or back to the main screen.

win screen: when the player reaches to the end point, the win screen will shows up, player can either go to next level or back to the main screen.

camera: the camera will be freeze in the beginning, when the player moves to near the middle of the screeen, the camera will be start following the player until they see the endpoint.

movement: player will use A and D to move left and right. They can also press Space to perform jump, and when the press space two times, it will perform a double jump. 

Leveling and Point: player can get exp and points/score by getting the collectibles, each collectible will have different points (points = the exp you will get to level up).

power ups: there will be also some power ups in the maps for player to collect, each power up will have different effect/buffs

 moving platforms: some of the platform will be moving back and forth.
